SOURCE ATTRIBUTION AND REDISTRIBUTION NOTICE

No original corpus text, extracted JSONL records, or derived training data are
included in this repository.

The Naaladiyar material used during corpus construction was obtained from the
Project Madurai digital library:
https://www.projectmadurai.org/

Project Madurai's distribution page asks third-party distributors to retain the
original header page, Project Madurai logo, and credit acknowledgements, and to
contact its coordinators before online redistribution. This repository therefore
provides parsing code only and does not redistribute Project Madurai text. See
`PROJECT_MADURAI_NOTICE.txt` for the header-preservation condition.

Thirukadukam and the Tholkappiyam source sections were obtained from the Tamil
Virtual University: https://www.tamilvu.org/. The Tholkappiyam material was
manually collected from that site. Tamil Virtual Academy confirmed that the
specified texts may be used for this academic research; the message does not
state redistribution permission. See `TAMILVU_ACADEMIC_USE_PERMISSION.txt`.
Users must therefore obtain the texts directly and verify the applicable terms
before creating or sharing a derived dataset.

Please cite and acknowledge Project Madurai and the Tamil Virtual University as
the digital sources of the texts used for extraction. This header does not grant
permission to redistribute any source text or derivative dataset.
