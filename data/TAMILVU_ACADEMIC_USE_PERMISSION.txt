TAMIL VIRTUAL UNIVERSITY / ACADEMY ACADEMIC-USE NOTE

On 20 August 2026, Tamil Virtual Academy replied to the researcher's request
about using Tamil texts available through https://www.tamilvu.org/ for the
academic research project. The reply states that the specified publicly
available Tamil texts may be used for academic research purposes.

This repository does not include the original Tamil Virtual University texts or
their extracted JSONL records. The reply does not explicitly grant permission
to redistribute source texts or derivative datasets; this release therefore
provides source attribution and code only.

For this study, the Tholkappiyam material was manually collected from the Tamil
Virtual University website. Please cite the Tamil Virtual University as the
digital source and obtain the text directly before reproducing the extraction.
